# Where does this number come from?

Every number and data-driven word the site shows (`site/index.html`, `site/js/app.js`, `site/js/features.js`, `site/js/how.js`), screen by screen in the order a visitor meets them, traced back to its dataset. Home now leads to four features (24-Year Replay, Field Pulse, Safe Trial, Data Check-up); the earlier screens sit under "More". Values are the demo field's on 2026-11-14 (commit 1762f04), with the rule switches as committed: the tuned rain is the central rain (`gpm_series.gauge_calibration` on), paddy losses are on (`paddy_losses`), the satellite season dates and rain-delayed rice are off (`season_dates`, `paddy_rainfed`: 4a and 4c undone), `field_capacity_measured` is off, and groundwater no longer changes the water score (`plans.water_strict_when_falling` = 1.0). Cells marked **Finding** are gaps a judge could find. The last two tables list the 17 earlier gaps with their status today, then the new ones.

## Farmer sentences, their outputs and labels (owner's rules, 2026-11-14 late evening)

Every sentence a farmer screen can show that comes from data, the file and key that feed it, the output whose label it
carries (`site/data/labels/demo.json`), and the wording that label allows (rules.yaml `farmer_answers.wording`: VERIFIED
a plain statement; ESTIMATE and NOT YET VERIFIABLE soft, "usually" or "may", never a plain promise). S =
`site/data/simple/demo.json`. The back end picks the text key (`pipeline/export/build_simple.py`); the site only renders
it. `tests/test_simple_cards.py` checks that no plain action or plan word is written while its output is not VERIFIED.
Fixed screen words with no data behind them (titles, buttons, "You know your land best.", the soil test's steps, the
Safe Trial sheet) carry no output and are not listed. How we know (judges only) shows every label and why.
