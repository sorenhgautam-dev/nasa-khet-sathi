# METHOD_NOTES.md: stated assumptions of the field twin

The twin is a simple model. These are the simplifications it makes on purpose, each with who approved it
and where it lives in the code. They feed the site's method page and each plan's "unknown" tab (build
step 7). Numbers and their sources are in `config/rules.yaml` and `config/crops.json`; checks are in
`VALIDATION.md`.
