# METHOD_NOTES.md: stated assumptions of the field twin

The twin is a simple model. These are the simplifications it makes on purpose, each with who approved it
and where it lives in the code. They feed the site's method page and each plan's "unknown" tab (build
step 7). Numbers and their sources are in `config/rules.yaml` and `config/crops.json`; checks are in
`VALIDATION.md`.

| 1 | **Bucket hand-over at a crop change.** Each crop (and each bare spell) has its own bucket depth, set by its rooting depth Zr. When one ends and the next begins, the root zone's dryness carries over as the same *fraction* of the water the soil can hold (TAW), not as the same millimetres. | Keeps every segment's water balance exact (residual 0.000 mm). It moves water on paper when the depth changes: the step-4 plot shows a jump in depletion on 18 March, when wheat's 1.5 m bucket hands over to the bare spell before rice, which uses rice's 1.0 m depth (a bare spell takes the next crop's depth) | Owner, step-4 review item 6, 2026-11-12 | `pipeline/twin/season.py` `carry_over` |
| 2 | **Paddy rice is ponded only when irrigated** (changed 2026-11-12, REVIEW_NOTES H1 and L1). Irrigated paddy: every day of the season the root zone is refilled to field capacity at the start of the day (no water stress); that water is irrigation, counted in `irrigation_mm`, with `pond_supply_mm` the paddy part (never extra water). Rain-fed paddy gets no outside water: the ordinary bucket with rice's Zr 1.0 m and p 0.20 (FAO-56 gives p as a fraction of saturation; the bucket applies it between field capacity and wilting point). No bund or standing water is modelled, so rain-fed rice is on the dry side. | FAO-56 gives no paddy water balance. The earlier rain-fed rule (ponded until the first 10-day dry spell) gave 120-203 mm a season of unsourced water, so rain-fed and irrigated rice were the same by construction. Rain-fed rice now feels dry spells (dry run 2017: 23 dry days, 98.3%), but a monsoon that is late before transplanting is still invisible (2015: 99%) | Owner, step-4 review item 5; changed under the accepted review (H1); owner-approved 2026-11-12 (owner's batch decisions, item 6 and 7 (rice p 0.20)) | `season.simulate`, `soil_water.run_bucket` |

| 16 | **Kc_mid and Kc_end are adjusted for each season's own climate** (FAO-56 eqs. 62 and 65; was: Table 12 values unadjusted). Table 12 is for a sub-humid climate (RHmin about 45%, wind 2 m/s). Kc_end is adjusted only above 0.45 (eq. 65). Inputs outside FAO-56's limits (wind 1-6 m/s, RHmin 20-80%) are held at the limit (our reading: FAO-56 says the limits 'should be observed' but gives no rule).
